# Phase 06.1 — Fashion Attribute Dictionary

## 1. Shared Vocabularies

### 1.1 Occasions
- `everyday`: Daily routine and casual activities.
- `casual`: Informal, relaxed social settings.
- `workwear`: Professional office wear.
- `business_formal`: Executive, boardroom, and corporate formal wear.
- `party`: Celebrations, social gatherings, clubwear.
- `wedding`: Wedding ceremonies and receptions.
- `festive`: Traditional Indian and cultural festivities (Diwali, Eid, Puja).
- `traditional_ceremony`: Religious rituals and ceremonies.
- `date_night`: Evening dates and dinners.
- `travel`: Comfortable transit and transit wear.
- `vacation`: Resort, holiday, and leisure wear.
- `sports`: Athletics and sports activities.
- `gym`: Workout and fitness training.
- `outdoor`: Hiking, camping, and outdoor recreation.
- `lounge`: Home lounging and sleepwear.
- `evening`: Formal galas and black-tie events.

### 1.2 Intended Audience
- `men`, `women`, `unisex`, `boys`, `girls`, `kids`, `infants`, `all_audiences`, `unknown`.

### 1.3 Color Families & Shades
- `black`: `black`, `jet_black`, `charcoal`.
- `white`: `white`, `pure_white`, `ivory`, `off_white`.
- `grey`: `grey`, `light_grey`, `dark_grey`, `heather_grey`.
- `brown`: `brown`, `tan`, `camel`, `chocolate`, `beige`.
- `red`: `red`, `maroon`, `burgundy`, `crimson`.
- `pink`: `pink`, `baby_pink`, `rose`, `magenta`.
- `orange`: `orange`, `rust`, `coral`, `peach`.
- `yellow`: `yellow`, `mustard`, `lemon`.
- `green`: `green`, `olive`, `sage`, `mint`, `emerald`.
- `blue`: `blue`, `navy`, `sky_blue`, `royal_blue`, `indigo`.
- `purple`: `purple`, `lavender`, `violet`, `plum`.
- `metallic`: `gold`, `silver`, `bronze`.
- `multicolor`, `other`.

### 1.4 Patterns
- **Universal:** `solid`, `striped`, `checked`, `plaid`, `floral`, `geometric`, `abstract`, `graphic`, `printed`, `embroidered`, `polka_dot`, `animal_print`, `camouflage`, `paisley`, `ikat`, `tie_dye`, `color_block`, `textured`, `self_design`.
- **Indian Craftsmanship:** `block_print`, `bandhani`, `ajrak`, `kalamkari`, `batik`, `leheriya`, `chikankari`, `phulkari`, `kantha`, `zari_work`.

### 1.5 Fabrics & Materials
- **Natural:** `cotton`, `organic_cotton`, `linen`, `silk`, `raw_silk`, `wool`, `cashmere`, `leather`.
- **Synthetic & Semi-synthetic:** `polyester`, `nylon`, `viscose`, `rayon`, `modal`, `lyocell`, `acrylic`, `faux_leather`.
- **Weave / Pile / Construction:** `denim`, `satin`, `chiffon`, `georgette`, `crepe`, `velvet`, `net`.

### 1.6 Construction Attributes
- **Sleeves:** `sleeveless`, `cap_sleeve`, `short_sleeve`, `half_sleeve`, `three_quarter_sleeve`, `full_sleeve`, `long_sleeve`, `roll_up_sleeve`, `bell_sleeve`, `puff_sleeve`, `bishop_sleeve`, `raglan_sleeve`.
- **Necklines:** `crew_neck`, `round_neck`, `v_neck`, `scoop_neck`, `boat_neck`, `square_neck`, `sweetheart_neck`, `halter_neck`, `high_neck`, `mock_neck`, `turtle_neck`, `collared`, `mandarin_collar`, `peter_pan_collar`, `off_shoulder`, `one_shoulder`.
- **Collars:** `spread_collar`, `button_down_collar`, `cutaway_collar`, `band_collar`, `notched_lapel`.
- **Fits:** `regular`, `slim`, `relaxed`, `oversized`, `fitted`, `tight`, `loose`.
- **Silhouettes:** `straight`, `a_line`, `fit_and_flare`, `bodycon`, `flared`, `wide_leg`, `tapered`, `bootcut`, `skinny`, `cropped`, `layered`, `asymmetric`.
