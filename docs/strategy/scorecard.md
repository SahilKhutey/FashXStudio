# Strategic Options Pre-Committed Scorecard

**Policy**: Criteria and weights were pre-committed prior to option scoring to prevent post-hoc rationalization.  
**Scoring Rule**: Each criterion is scored on a 1–5 scale accompanied by one concise sentence of empirical evidence. Weighted Total = $\sum (\text{Score}_i \times \text{Weight}_i)$. In accordance with Section 11.6, any difference of less than 10% (35 points out of 350 max) is treated as a tie; ties resolve in favor of the option with higher learning speed and lower capital cost.

---

## 1. Pre-Committed Criteria & Weights

| Criterion | Weight | Definition of a 5 ("Gold Standard") |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Evidence from Pilot** | **25** | Strong, direct measured empirical support for the path's core assumption. |
| **2. Defensibility vs. Free Tech** | **20** | Specific, hard-to-copy moat: proprietary fit data, brand contracts, localized silhouette expertise. |
| **3. Path to 12-Month Revenue** | **15** | A paying customer with high willingness to pay and a proven, short sales motion. |
| **4. Capital & Effort Required** | **10** | Achievable by a solo technical founder and one GTM co-founder with minimal external capital. |
| **5. Risk & Platform Independence** | **10** | Minimal single points of failure, zero vulnerability to affiliate network policy terminations. |
| **6. Founder Fit & Co-Founder Role** | **10** | Aligns with technical strengths; creates a crystal-clear, high-ownership role for a non-tech co-founder. |
| **7. Learning Speed (90-Day Cycle)**| **10** | Definitive proof or kill signal can be obtained within a 90-day execution window. |
| **Total Weight** | **100** | Maximum Score: 500 points |

## 2. Option Scoring & Empirical Evidence

### Option A: Consumer-First (B2C App with Affiliate Revenue)
- **1. Evidence from Pilot (Score: 2/5)**: Pilot proved engagement (68.4% W2 return) but demonstrated commercially fatal affiliate economics (-₹33/user/month net contribution).
- **2. Defensibility (Score: 2/5)**: Google's free try-on on Google Shopping directly targets generic consumer apparel browsing.
- **3. Path to Revenue (Score: 1/5)**: Affiliate networks pay 30–60 days late with micro-payouts (~₹144/order) that fail to cover marginal inference costs.
- **4. Capital & Effort (Score: 2/5)**: Consumer acquisition in India requires massive ad spend or sustained creator budgets exceeding bootstrap runway.
- **5. Risk Profile (Score: 2/5)**: Highly vulnerable to affiliate program unilateral cancellation (e.g. Flipkart's termination clause) and consumer novelty churn.
- **6. Founder Fit (Score: 3/5)**: Fun consumer product to build, but co-founder role becomes high-burn consumer growth hacking.
- **7. Learning Speed (Score: 3/5)**: Validating long-term consumer retention takes 6–9 months of cohort monitoring.
- **Weighted Total**: $(2\times25) + (2\times20) + (1\times15) + (2\times10) + (2\times10) + (3\times10) + (3\times10) = 50 + 40 + 15 + 20 + 20 + 30 + 30 =$ **205 / 500**

### Option B: Brand-First B2B (Fit & Try-On Widget for Indian D2C Brands)
- **1. Evidence from Pilot (Score: 4/5)**: Technical accuracy on Indian sizing and ethnic drape (+0.75 over Google) directly matches brand return pain points.
- **2. Defensibility (Score: 4/5)**: Proprietary brand-specific size calibration ledger and direct merchant storefront integration cannot be displaced by Google's general search ads.
- **3. Path to Revenue (Score: 5/5)**: Direct B2B willingness to pay confirmed by 5 brand founders at ₹15,000–₹25,000/month to solve a ₹350/return P&L drain.
- **4. Capital & Effort (Score: 4/5)**: B2B embeddable Shopify widget leverages existing backend pipelines with zero consumer ad acquisition costs.
- **5. Risk Profile (Score: 4/5)**: Contractual SaaS revenue with zero dependence on affiliate networks or third-party cookies.
- **6. Founder Fit (Score: 5/5)**: Ideal division of labor: technical founder owns SDK/ML pipelines, GTM co-founder owns founder-led B2B brand sales.
- **7. Learning Speed (Score: 5/5)**: A 30-day A/B split pilot on 3 Shopify stores provides definitive return reduction metrics within 60 days.
- **Weighted Total**: $(4\times25) + (4\times20) + (5\times15) + (4\times10) + (4\times10) + (5\times10) + (5\times10) = 100 + 80 + 75 + 40 + 40 + 50 + 50 =$ **435 / 500**

### Option C: Hybrid Model (Consumer App + Brand Drop Partnerships & Fit Analytics)
- **1. Evidence from Pilot (Score: 4/5)**: Consumer retention (68.4%) and fit survey satisfaction (79.5%) validate consumer engagement, while brand demand supports paid placement.
- **2. Defensibility (Score: 4/5)**: Combines direct consumer preference data with merchant brand relationships, creating a two-sided network.
- **3. Path to Revenue (Score: 3/5)**: Brand drop fees (₹25,000) are viable, but requires maintaining consumer app scale simultaneously with B2B sales.
- **4. Capital & Effort (Score: 2/5)**: Running two distinct operations (consumer mobile app + B2B brand relationships) strains a lean 2-person founding team.
- **5. Risk Profile (Score: 3/5)**: Brand sponsorships risk compromising consumer feed trust if not strictly partitioned.
- **6. Founder Fit (Score: 4/5)**: Broad mandate, but stretches co-founder across both consumer community marketing and enterprise brand sales.
- **7. Learning Speed (Score: 3/5)**: Requires testing two distinct flywheels concurrently, slowing 90-day iteration speed.
- **Weighted Total**: $(4\times25) + (4\times20) + (3\times15) + (2\times10) + (3\times10) + (4\times10) + (3\times10) = 100 + 80 + 45 + 20 + 30 + 40 + 30 =$ **345 / 500**
