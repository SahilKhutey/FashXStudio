## 2. Candidate Profile & Rubric

### Must-Have Qualifications:
- **Direct Domain Exposure**: Has sold to, partnered with, or led growth inside Indian fashion or e-commerce D2C brands (2+ years experience in B2B SaaS sales, agency growth, or marketplace category management).
- **Rolodex & Access**: Able to immediately secure 10+ warm conversations with Indian fashion brand founders or heads of e-commerce.
- **Relentless Work Ethic**: High comfort with early-stage ambiguity, unglamorous cold outbound, manual onboarding, and rejection.
- **Full-Time Availability**: Clear path to committing 100% full-time upon successful completion of the paid trial project.

### Strong Positive Signals:
- Can walk through a specific B2B enterprise deal or merchant contract they personally originated, negotiated, and closed.
- Deep, intuitive understanding of Indian e-commerce unit economics (RTO, forward/reverse shipping costs, COD risks, Shopify tech stack).
- High intellectual curiosity about generative AI and computer vision workflows.

### Red Flags (Instant Disqualification):
- Demands 50% equity upfront before executing any hands-on deliverables.
- Talks exclusively about venture capital, valuations, and press releases rather than customer pain and return reduction.
- Cannot name five specific Indian D2C fashion brands experiencing high return rates.
- Unwilling to participate in a structured paid trial project.

## 4. Structured Evaluation & 4-Week Paid Trial Process

*Rule: Never commit to a co-founder without working together under pressure on a real commercial deliverable.*

```
[30-Min Intro Call] ──► [2-Hour Data & Strategy Session] ──► [4-Week Paid Trial Project] ──► [2x References] ──► [Founders' Agreement]
```

### Stage 1: Screening Conversation (30 min)
- Motivation, commercial background, why fashion tech in India, risk tolerance.

### Stage 2: Deep-Dive Working Session (2 hours)
- Present the Phase 10 pilot numbers and brand interview transcripts.
- Co-design an outbound campaign for 10 target brands. Observe how feedback is received and how disagreements are navigated.

### Stage 3: The Paid Trial Project (4 Weeks)
- **Clear Measurable Deliverable**: *"Secure at least 3 qualified, scheduled discovery calls with target D2C fashion brand founders, and co-pitch 1 design-partner pilot agreement."*
- **Compensation**: Fixed fair stipend (**₹35,000 to ₹50,000 INR** for the 4-week trial).
- **Assessment Criteria**: Speed of execution, communication clarity, resilience when brands say no, and cultural alignment.

### Stage 4: Confidential Reference Checks
- Conduct confidential calls with at least two individuals who previously managed or co-founded with the candidate.

## 5. Equity, Vesting & Founders' Agreement Terms

*(To be formalized by outside startup counsel and Company Secretary upon entity incorporation)*

- **Vesting Schedule**: Standard **4-Year Vesting with a 1-Year Cliff**, monthly vesting thereafter.
- **Equity Split Framework**:
  - Technical Founder (You): **55% – 65%** (accounting for 10 phases of completed architecture, working code, IP, pilot data, and initial capital).
  - Commercial Co-Founder: **35% – 45%** (reflecting full-time forward commitment, sales ownership, and long-term value creation).
  - 10% Unallocated ESOP pool created upon incorporation for early engineering hires.
- **Core Contractual Clauses**:
  - **IP Assignment**: Explicit, irreversible assignment of all pre-existing and future code, designs, data, and trademarks to the company entity.
  - **Deadlock Resolution**: Technical founder retains casting vote on technical/product architecture; commercial co-founder holds casting vote on sales pricing tactics; joint agreement required for board/financing decisions.
  - **Leaver Provisions**: Standard Good Leaver / Bad Leaver clawback mechanisms protecting the company if either founder departs early.
