# Corporate, Legal & Compliance Housekeeping Checklist

**Governing Entity Objective**: Transition from individual developer project to a properly structured Indian Private Limited company (`FashXStudio Private Limited` or approved variant).  
**Ownership**: Technical Founder (Interim Lead) $\to$ Commercial Co-Founder (Post-Trial).

---

## 1. Corporate Governance & Entity Formation

| Item | Action Required | Status | Target Date | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Name Availability Search** | Conduct MCA name availability search for "FASHXSTUDIO TECHNOLOGIES" on Ministry of Corporate Affairs portal. | **SCHEDULED** | Day 30 | Tech Founder |
| **Trademark Search & Filing** | Search Indian Trademark Registry (Class 9 - Software, Class 42 - SaaS); file TM application for "FashXStudio". | **SCHEDULED** | Day 35 | Outside IP Counsel |
| **Pvt Ltd Incorporation** | Incorporate Private Limited entity via SPICe+ form with MCA (2 directors, 2 shareholders). | **SCHEDULED** | Day 45 (Post Co-Founder Trial) | Company Secretary (CS) |
| **DPIIT Recognition** | File for Startup India recognition via startupindia.gov.in (enables Section 80-IAC tax holiday & SISFS eligibility). | **SCHEDULED** | Day 50 | CS / Co-Founder |
| **Corporate Bank Account** | Open current account with automated banking APIs (ICICI / HDFC / RazorpayX). | **SCHEDULED** | Day 55 | Commercial Co-Founder |
| **GST Registration** | Obtain Goods and Services Tax (GST) registration for B2B SaaS invoicing in home state. | **SCHEDULED** | Day 60 (Prior to First B2B Invoice) | Chartered Accountant (CA) |
