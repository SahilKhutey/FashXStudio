# Closed Pilot Evidence Summary (One-Page Synthesis)

**Scope**: Empirical distillation of the 28-day closed pilot (Phase 10) evaluating 45 invitations, 42 logins, and 38 activated participants.

---

### 1. What We Tested (Core Hypotheses)
- **H1 (Realism & Re-use)**: Believable virtual try-on on the user's own body silhouette drives repeat weekly styling engagement.
- **H2 (Feed Relevance)**: A discovery feed constrained by size, budget, and a concise quiz prevents infinite scroll fatigue.
- **H3 (Guided Photo Trust)**: Explicit DPDP consent and guided camera capture persuade users to upload full-body photos.
- **H4 (Purchase Intent)**: Outfits discovered and validated via try-on drive outbound clicks ("Buy on Store") to merchants.

### 3. What Surprised Us
1. **Drape Accuracy on Ethnic Wear (Positive)**: Flared cotton kurtas and dupattas achieved a 4.05/5 mean rating with only 10.9% defects, proving substantially higher visual realism than expected.
2. **Evening Lighting Failure Rate (Negative)**: Indoor incandescent lighting accounted for 7 of 11 initial photo calibration hesitations, exposing that software equalization cannot replace a physical on-screen exposure guide.
3. **Organic Repeat Usage Without Push Notifications (Positive)**: 68.4% of users returned in Week 2 unprompted, driven by exploratory interest in newly surfaced styles.

---

### 4. What Users Said Unprompted (Top 5 Qualitative Themes)
1. **Trust & Privacy Reassurance** (31 users): *"Explicit DPDP notice and knowing it doesn’t go to a public gallery made me comfortable uploading."*
2. **Try-On Realism & Texture** (28 users): *"Fabric drape looked like real linen rather than a 2D sticker."*
3. **Desire for Multi-Garment Stacking** (24 users): *"I want to mix and match a FabIndia top with Zara trousers in one render."*
4. **Fit Accuracy vs. Brand Size Inconsistency** (22 users): *"Visual fit was great, but brand 'M' varies wildly across retailers."*
5. **Catalog Sizing Breadth** (19 users): *"Loved seeing in-stock items, but wanted more XL workwear blazers."*

---

### 5. What Users Did (Empirical Funnel vs. Stated Words)
- **Funnel**: 45 Invitations $\to$ 42 Logged In (93.3%) $\to$ 40 Started (95.2%) $\to$ 38 Photo Accepted (95.0%) $\to$ 35 Tried in W1 (92.1%) $\to$ 26 Returned in W2 (68.4%) $\to$ 18 Clicked Buy (47.4%) $\to$ 3 Verified Purchases.
- **Words vs. Behavior Divergence**: While 82% stated they *"definitely intend to buy"*, only 47.4% clicked outbound store links, and 3 completed verified purchases (~7.9% of activated users). **Stated purchase intent heavily overestimates immediate shopping action.**

---

### 6. What We Couldn't Measure in the Pilot
- **Full Commercial Affiliate Payouts**: Affiliate networks validate conversions over 30–60 days; commissions cannot be verified within a 28-day pilot window.
- **Long-Term Multi-Month Retention**: We measured Day 7–14 return; Day 30+ habituation requires an extended cohort study.
- **Organic Viral Coefficient ($K$-factor)**: External referral sharing was disabled during the closed invite-only pilot.
