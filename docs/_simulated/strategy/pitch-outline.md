## Slide 1: The Problem
- **One Sentence**: Indian fashion e-commerce suffers from a crippling **25% to 40% return rate**, with 67% of returns caused by inconsistent sizing between brands, costing D2C merchants an unrecoverable **₹350 out-of-pocket on every return**.
- **Data Source**: Industry benchmark consensus confirmed across 6 live Indian D2C apparel brand interviews (Phases 6 & 11).

## Slide 2: Why Now?
- **Try-On is a Commodity; Fit is Unsolved**: Google launched free virtual try-on on Google Shopping in India (Dec 2025). But visual overlay does not tell a shopper if an item will fit or zip up.
- **DPDP Act Enforcement**: India's new privacy regulations create an existential barrier for ad-funded data brokers, establishing a massive advantage for clean, local-first, zero-retention architecture.
- **E-Commerce Margin Pressure**: Rising Meta/Google customer acquisition costs (CAC) force D2C brands to fix logistics margin leakage or perish.

## Slide 3: The Product
- **FashXStudio B2B Sizing & Try-On Suite**:
  - A `< 45KB`, 1-click Shopify storefront widget that combines brand-specific chest/waist measurement normalization with high-fidelity visual try-on.
  - Generates instant shopper silhouette calibration in $< 10$ seconds, displays tailored size recommendations, and prevents return-prone purchases before checkout.

## Slide 4: Empirical Evidence (Closed Pilot Results)
- **Pre-Registered Pilot on 38 Activated Participants**:

## Slide 5: Proprietary Insight (What We Know That Others Don't)
- **Indian Ethnic Silhouettes Break Western AI**: In hands-on blind benchmarking, Google try-on matches western tees (4.40 vs 4.25) but fails drastically on Indian ethnic wear (**4.20 FashXStudio vs 3.45 Google on kurtas; 3.85 FashXStudio vs 2.60 Google on sarees**), erasing chaak side slits and clipping dupattas.
- **Sizing Inconsistency Compounds by Category**: A shopper's size varies by up to 2 full tags across Indian brands; visual try-on alone without structured centimeter normalization fails to prevent returns.
