## 1. Investment Thesis & Key Pitch Highlights

1. **Massive Quantified Pain Point:** Indian fashion ecommerce suffers from 25–40% return rates, with 70% driven by sizing and drape mismatch. Brands lose ₹350 out-of-pocket on every return.
2. **Proven ROI & Moat:** 5 enterprise D2C brands live; verified **3.4 percentage points return rate reduction** generating ₹6.80 in merchant savings per ₹1.00 paid.
3. **Indian Garment Visual Superiority:** Blind benchmarks prove +0.75 score advantage over Google Shopping try-on for Sarees and Kurtas.
4. **Clean Statutory Posture:** Ephemeral 24h photo architecture fully compliant with India's DPDP Act 2023 / Rules 2025.
