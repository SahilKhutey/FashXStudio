# RC13: Market Landscape & Competitive Analysis

## 1. Target Market: India Fashion E-Commerce
- **Market Dynamics**:
  - Rapidly expanding online apparel shopper base in Tier 1 and Tier 2 Indian cities.
  - High return rates (25–40%) across Indian fashion e-commerce platforms primarily driven by fit, sizing, and look uncertainty.
  - Increasing smartphone camera quality and digital payment penetration.
- **Value Proposition**:
  - Empower shoppers to visualize garments on themselves before purchasing, reducing return rates and boosting buyer confidence.

## 2. Competitive Landscape
- **Global VTO Players**: Large horizontal tech platforms and standalone VTO SDKs. Often lack localization, regional clothing styles (e.g. ethnic wear, kurtas), or direct retailer handoffs in India.
- **Domestic Aggregators**: Native marketplace apps with basic size charts or synthetic avatar models. Few provide zero-biometric photo-realistic try-on directly across multi-brand selections.
- **FashXStudio Differentiator**:
  - Zero biometric storage & ephemeral photo lifecycle.
  - Multi-retailer curated catalog with direct try-on visualization.
  - Lightweight mobile app optimized for diverse mobile networks.
