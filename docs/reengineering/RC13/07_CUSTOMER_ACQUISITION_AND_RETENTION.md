# RC13: Customer Acquisition, Onboarding & Retention

## 1. Funnel Architecture
1. **Acquisition**: Invite codes, partner merchant links, fashion community referrals.
2. **Onboarding & Consent**:
   - Transparent, plain-language DPDP consent explaining that user photos are used solely to generate try-ons and are never sold or trained on.
3. **First Try-On Activation**:
   - Guided camera capture flow with pose framing guidelines.
   - Recommended starter catalog for instant try-on within 60 seconds of registration.
4. **Retention Loop**:
   - Saved wardrobe / lookbook history.
   - Weekly catalog additions in favorite categories.
   - Target Week 2 return rate >= 20%.

## 2. Friction Mitigation
- No forced account creation before browsing catalog.
- One-click photo deletion directly in mobile app settings.
- Fast try-on queue status updates and cached results.
