# RC08 — Legal, Partner & Vendor Readiness

## 1. Compliance Baseline: Indian DPDP Act (2023)
- **Lawful Grounds for Processing**: Explicit, affirmative, itemized consent obtained prior to processing user biometric/body images.
- **Purpose Limitation**: Personal photos are utilized exclusively for the requested virtual try-on session; no unauthorized model retraining.
- **Right to Erasure & Grievance Redressal**: One-click account and data erasure (`DELETE /api/v1/me`) and published Data Protection Officer contact details.
- **Legal Advisory Requirement**: Staging consent text and privacy policy must be vetted by licensed legal counsel before onboarding external users.

## 2. Retailer & Affiliate Terms
- **Handoff Model**: FashX is a discovery and fit confidence assistant. Commercial transactions occur entirely on retailer portals.
- **Image Display Clearance**: Retailer images must have explicit affiliate permissions or documented fair-use grounds.
- **Affiliate Attribution**: Destination links must include approved `utm_source=fashx` tags without cloaking destination URLs.

## 3. Virtual Try-On Vendor Terms
- Commercial service agreement with Fashn.ai in place.
- Data Processing Addendum (DPA) confirming zero permanent retention of personal photos on third-party inference infrastructure.
