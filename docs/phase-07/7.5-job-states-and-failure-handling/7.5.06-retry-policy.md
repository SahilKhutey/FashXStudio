# Phase 07.5.06 — Operation-Specific Retry Policies, Exponential Backoff, and Jitter

## 1. Overview
Retries must be tailored to the specific operation failure, not applied blindly to the entire job. A retry budget for downloading output bytes should not be consumed by queue dispatch or S3 upload retries.

FashXStudio defines independent retry policies in `backend/fashx/tryon/lifecycle/retry_policy.py`.

---

## 2. Operation-Specific Retry Budgets

| Operation | Max Retries | Initial Delay ($D_0$) | Max Delay ($D_{\max}$)| Jitter Enabled? | Safe to Repeat? |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Queue Dispatch** | 8 | $2.0\text{ s}$ | $300.0\text{ s}$ | Yes | Yes (Idempotent outbox) |
| **Worker Execution** (pre-inference)| 3 | $5.0\text{ s}$ | $120.0\text{ s}$ | Yes | Yes (Checks active lease) |
| **Provider Polling** | 8 | $3.0\text{ s}$ | $60.0\text{ s}$ | Yes | Yes (GET status is idempotent) |
| **Output Download** | 4 | $5.0\text{ s}$ | $60.0\text{ s}$ | Yes | Yes (Same output URL) |
| **S3 Storage Upload** | 5 | $2.0\text{ s}$ | $60.0\text{ s}$ | Yes | Yes (Deterministic key) |
| **Provider Inference POST** | **0** | — | — | — | **No (Unknown outcome requires reconciliation)** |

## 3. Exponential Backoff Formula with Jitter

For retry attempt $n \in \{0, 1, 2, \dots\}$:

$$D_n = \min\Big(D_{\max},\; D_0 \times 2^n\Big)$$

To eliminate the "thundering herd" problem where multiple retrying jobs hit upstream services simultaneously, uniform random jitter is applied:

$$D_{\text{actual}} = \mathcal{U}\Big(0.5 \times D_n,\; D_n\Big)$$

---

## 4. Provider `Retry-After` Adherence

When an upstream provider returns an HTTP 429 response with a `Retry-After: <seconds>` header:
- The backend parses the delay $t_{\text{retry\_after}}$.
- **Never retry earlier than requested**:
  $$\text{base} = \max\Big(D_n,\; t_{\text{retry\_after}}\Big)$$
- Bounded only by the maximum operational deferral limit ($1800\text{ s} = 30\text{ minutes}$).
- If provider instructs a delay longer than 30 minutes, the job is flagged for operator triage rather than prematurely violating rate limits.
