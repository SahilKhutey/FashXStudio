## 2. Slice Performance Analysis

Ratings evaluated across Monk skin tone categories, body types, and garment classes:

| Slice Dimension | Sample Size | Mean Overall (1-5) | Acceptability (≥4) | Delta vs Global (4.12) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Skin Tone: Monk 2–4 (Light)** | 24 | 4.21 | 79.2% | +0.09 |
| **Skin Tone: Monk 5–7 (Medium / South Asian)** | 32 | 4.14 | 78.1% | +0.02 |
| **Skin Tone: Monk 8–10 (Deep)** | 24 | 4.01 | 70.8% | -0.11 |
| **Body Type: Small / Medium** | 46 | 4.18 | 78.3% | +0.06 |
| **Body Type: Large / Plus** | 34 | 4.04 | 73.5% | -0.08 |
| **Garment: Western Tops** | 30 | 4.28 | 83.3% | +0.16 |
| **Garment: Western Bottoms** | 20 | 4.10 | 75.0% | -0.02 |
| **Garment: Indian Kurtas / Shirts** | 16 | 4.06 | 75.0% | -0.06 |
| **Garment: Unanchored Draped Drapes (Sarees / Lehengas)** | 14 | 3.84 | 57.1% | **-0.28 (Gated)** |

> **Capability Gating Consequence (Step 5.20):** Complex unanchored drape formats (e.g. Sarees and Lehengas with floating dupattas) underperformed structured apparel. Accordingly, capability gating is enforced in production: only `tops`, `bottoms`, and structured `one-pieces` are enabled (`tryon_supported = false` on complex draped ethnic wear).
