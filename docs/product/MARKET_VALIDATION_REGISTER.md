## 1. Research Objectives & Methodology

The goal of this research stream is to empirically validate the 15 core product hypotheses defined in [docs/product/PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md](file:///c:/Users/ASUS/Documents/FashXStudio/docs/product/PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md#12-assumptions-register-version-10) before committing extensive capital to full-scale enterprise integrations.

### Research Streams
1. **Merchant Discovery Track:** 8–12 Indian D2C apparel brand founders, e-commerce leads, and merchandise managers.
2. **Shopper Discovery Track:** 15–20 active Indian online fashion shoppers (men and women aged 18–35, covering Western and Ethnic apparel).
3. **Interactive Prototype Validation:** Usability testing of the Expo mobile app and web widget against synthetic test catalogs.

## 2. Merchant Discovery Interview Guide

*Interviewer Rule:* Conduct neutral discovery. Do not pitch FashXStudio during the first 20 minutes. Focus entirely on the merchant's unit economics, return rates, sizing complaints, and integration bottlenecks.

| ID | Discovery Question | Investigation Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **M01** | What are your primary customer acquisition and discovery channels today? | Assess current marketing CAC and reliance on Meta/Google Ads. |
| **M02** | What are your biggest customer drop-off points on product detail pages (PDPs)? | Identify appearance and fit hesitation. |
| **M03** | How frequently do shoppers contact customer support regarding sizing or appearance? | Quantify customer sizing anxiety. |
| **M04** | What percentage of your catalog has standardized, garment-level size charts? | Evaluate merchant data readiness for fit normalization. |
| **M05** | Do you own full commercial copyrights and digital reproduction rights to your product imagery? | Establish legal clearance for AI diffusion try-on. |
| **M06** | Have you evaluated or piloted virtual try-on or 3D sizing solutions previously? | Uncover prior vendor experience and dissatisfaction drivers. |
| **M07** | Would allowing shoppers to visualize outfits on their own photos be valuable on your PDP? | Test willingness to deploy self-image VTO. |
| **M08** | What technical, branding, or operational barriers would prevent you from installing a VTO widget? | Identify adoption friction (e.g., site speed, brand aesthetic). |
| **M09** | What integration format works best for your team (Shopify App, REST API, JS snippet, feed)? | Determine platform integration priority. |
| **M10** | What concrete business outcomes (e.g. +10% conversion, -15% returns) would justify paying for VTO? | Pinpoint willingness-to-pay triggers. |
| **M11** | Would you prefer a flat monthly SaaS subscription or a pay-per-completed-try-on pricing model? | Validate monetization preference. |
| **M12** | Would your team be open to participating in a controlled 4-week beta pilot on 100–300 SKUs? | Gauge commercial pilot commitment. |

## 3. Shopper Discovery Interview Guide

| ID | Discovery Question | Investigation Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **U01** | How often do you buy fashion items online, and what was your last clothing purchase? | Establish shopping frequency and recency. |
| **U02** | Which shopping apps or websites do you browse most frequently, and why? | Profile platform habits and multi-app comparison behavior. |
| **U03** | What is the single biggest factor that causes you to abandon a garment in your cart? | Isolate appearance/fit uncertainty vs pricing/shipping. |
| **U04** | How often do you receive clothes that do not fit properly or look different from the photos? | Quantify return frequency and appearance mismatch. |
| **U05** | How do you currently judge whether an item (e.g., a kurta or shirt) will suit your skin tone and body shape? | Uncover existing mental models and visual heuristics. |
| **U06** | Do studio model photos give you confidence that a garment will look good on you specifically? | Measure skepticism toward idealized studio photography. |
| **U07** | If you could see an outfit realistically rendered on a photo of yourself, would that help you decide? | Test baseline appetite for self-image VTO. |
| **U08** | What concerns, if any, would you have about uploading a full-length photograph to a fashion app? | Map privacy, security, and DPDP consent sensitivities. |
| **U09** | What would make you trust that an AI-generated try-on result is realistic rather than a gimmick? | Define photorealism and fabric drape quality thresholds. |
| **U10** | Would you find value in having your body measurements saved in one place for multiple brands? | Test portable personal profile thesis. |
| **U11** | Would you prefer browsing multiple brands in one app, or using try-on directly on each brand's site? | Validate B2C app vs B2B2C widget utility. |
| **U12** | What feature would make you return to FashXStudio on a weekly basis? | Identify long-term retention hooks (e.g. style alerts, wardrobe). |

## 4. Prototype Usability & Behavioral Testing Protocol

Participants are given access to a staging build of the FashXStudio mobile application and asked to complete three tasks without assistance:

1. **Task 1 (Discovery):** Search for and select a casual upper-body garment (shirt or kurta) they would consider purchasing.
2. **Task 2 (Virtual Try-On):** Use their own photo (or select a pre-loaded consenting model) to generate an AI try-on visualization.
3. **Task 3 (Action):** Decide whether to save the generated look to their personal wardrobe, retry with a different size/color, or click the merchant handoff link.

### Observational Metrics to Record (Per Session)
- Time to complete profile setup (seconds).
- Time to first try-on submission (seconds).
- Number of try-on retries requested.
- Qualitative confusion points (navigation, consent language, image guidelines).
- Subjective realism rating (1–5 scale).
- Stated intent to purchase via merchant link (Yes / No).
