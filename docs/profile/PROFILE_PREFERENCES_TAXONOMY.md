# Profile Preferences Taxonomy

## 1. Size Systems (`SizeSystem`)
- `IN`: Indian sizing standard (e.g. 38, 40, 42).
- `US`: United States standard (e.g. 4, 6, 8 / 30, 32, 34).
- `UK`: United Kingdom standard (e.g. 8, 10, 12).
- `EU`: European continental sizing (e.g. 36, 38, 40).
- `INTL`: Alpha standard (XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL).

## 2. Preferred Fit (`PreferredFit`)
- `SLIM`: Form-fitting silhouette with minimal ease.
- `REGULAR`: Classic cut balanced between structure and comfort.
- `RELAXED`: Generous drape and ease across torso and limbs.
- `OVERSIZED`: Dropped shoulder, intentionally spacious street aesthetic.
- `NO_PREFERENCE`: Shopper welcomes diverse silhouettes.

## 3. Style Preferences (`StylePreference`)
- `CASUAL`: Easy-going daywear, denim, t-shirts, sneakers.
- `FORMAL`: Tailored suits, dress shirts, evening gowns.
- `SMART_CASUAL`: Refined polish bridging work and leisure.
- `MINIMAL`: Monochromatic, clean lines, understated accents.
- `STREETWEAR`: Contemporary graphic urban wear, hoodies, cargo.
- `SPORTSWEAR`: Performance activewear, technical fabrics.
- `ETHNIC`: Indian and regional traditional wear (Kurtas, Sarees, Lehengas).
- `TRADITIONAL`: Classic heritage garments.
- `FUSION`: Indo-western and cross-cultural aesthetics.
- `PARTY`: High-energy celebratory and cocktail attire.
- `NO_PREFERENCE`: Versatile curation.

## 4. Color Palette Preferences (`ColorPreference`)
- Core Neutrals: `BLACK`, `WHITE`, `GREY`, `NAVY`, `BEIGE`, `BROWN`.
- Vibrant Accents: `RED`, `BLUE`, `GREEN`, `YELLOW`, `ORANGE`, `PINK`, `PURPLE`, `MAROON`.
- Blends: `MULTICOLOR`, `NO_PREFERENCE`.

## 5. Shopping Intents (`ShoppingIntent`)
- `EVERYDAY_WEAR`: Daily lifestyle and casual errands.
- `WORKWEAR`: Professional office environments.
- `COLLEGE_WEAR`: Campus and university student styling.
- `OCCASION_WEAR`: Weddings, galas, and festive milestones.
- `FESTIVE_WEAR`: Traditional holiday celebrations (Diwali, Eid, weddings).
- `SPORTSWEAR`: Athletic training and workouts.
- `TRAVEL_WEAR`: Vacation and resort itineraries.
