# Pilot Qualitative Interview Guide (30-Minute Protocol)

**Target Sample**: 8–10 Participants (deliberately including heavy users, light users, and 2 drop-offs).  
**Format**: 1-on-1 audio/video call (30 minutes).  
**Data Protection Rule**: Ask for permission to take notes. Do NOT record audio or video unless explicitly consented in writing. Notes stored pseudonymous (no names).  

---

## 1. Interview Principles
- **Ground in Behavior, Not Opinions**: Ask about what they *did* recently, not hypothetical thoughts.
- **Never Defend the Product**: If they criticize a feature, don't explain how it's supposed to work; say *"Tell me more about that"*.
- **Listen for Hesitations**: Pauses around the photo upload or body silhouette reveal deep trust dynamics.

---

## 2. Interview Protocol & Questions

### Warm-Up (2 minutes)
> *"Thank you so much for taking the time to chat! We’re working hard to make FashX genuinely helpful, and your blunt, unfiltered feedback is the only way we learn. There are no right or wrong answers, and you won't hurt my feelings at all."*

---

### Recent Behavior (8 minutes)
1. **"Walk me through the last time you opened the app."**
   - *Follow-ups*: Where were you? What prompted you to open it? What was the first thing you tapped?
2. **"What did you expect when you first accepted the invitation, and what actually happened when you tried it?"**

---

### The Photo & Privacy Step (6 minutes)
3. **"Take me back to when you took or uploaded your body photo. How did that step feel?"**
   - *Follow-ups*: Was there any moment where you hesitated or almost closed the app? What made you decide to proceed?
4. **"How did you feel about how your photos and data are handled?"**
   - *Follow-ups*: Did the explanation on the screen make sense? Did you look at the privacy settings?

---

### Virtual Try-On Realism (6 minutes)
5. **"Tell me about a specific try-on outfit you remember looking at."**
   - *Follow-ups*: What looked right to you? What looked unnatural or off? Did it actually look like *you*, or like someone else?
6. **"Did you try on items you would normally wear, or something different? How did the fabric and fit feel to you?"**

---

### Commerce & Purchase Intent (5 minutes)
7. **"Did anything you saw make you want to buy it?"**
   - *Follow-ups*: If yes, did you tap 'Buy on Store'? If you didn't buy, what stopped you? (Price, fit uncertainty, wrong brand, shipping?)
8. **"How did the clothing recommendations compare to what you usually shop for on Myntra, Ajio, or in stores?"**

---

### Wrap-Up & Retention (3 minutes)
9. **"What was the single most annoying or missing thing in the app?"**
10. **"What would make you open this app every week when looking for clothes? And what would make you delete it?"**

---
