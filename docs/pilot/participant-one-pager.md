# Welcome to the FashXStudio Pilot — Participant One-Pager

**Welcome!** You are one of ~45 people invited to test FashXStudio before our public release. This sheet explains everything you need to know.

---

## 1. What is FashXStudio?
FashXStudio is an AI-powered styling and virtual dressing room app. It lets you see how real, buyable garments look on your own body silhouette so you can shop with confidence.

---

## 2. Quick-Start Guide (First 10 Minutes)
1. **Sign In**: Enter your email address to receive a fast 6-digit one-time code (no passwords).
2. **Redeem Invite**: Enter the single-use invite code from your email.
3. **Capture Your Silhouette**: Snap a full-body self-portrait. Tips:
   - Stand 6–8 feet away from the camera in good, even lighting.
   - Wear form-fitting or everyday clothes (e.g., t-shirt and jeans).
   - Keep your head, arms, and feet clearly in frame.
4. **Browse & Try On**: Scroll through curated styles in your sizes. Tap **"Try On"** on any garment to see an AI render in ~6–8 seconds.
5. **Save or Shop**: Save looks to your virtual closet, or tap **"Buy on Store"** to view the item on the merchant's official site.

---

## 3. What to Expect (Pre-Release Software)
- FashX is an early preview build. You may occasionally encounter minor rendering artifacts on complex fabrics or brief load delays.
- Tops, dresses, and simple bottoms render with highest accuracy. Loose sarees or complex layered outerwear may look softer around the edges.

---

## 4. Privacy & Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)
- **Zero AI Foundation Training**: We never sell your photos and never use them to train public AI models.
- **Auto-Cleanup**: Unsaved try-ons automatically disappear after 30 days.
- **Instant Deletion**: Want your data erased? Tap **Profile > Settings > Privacy & Rights > Delete Account**. All your photos and records are instantly purged.
- **Indian Cloud**: All photos are stored in secure AWS data centers in Mumbai (`ap-south-1`).

---

## 5. Giving Feedback & Getting Help
- Found a bug or had a weird result? Tap the **"Report a Problem"** button in Settings or beneath any try-on result.
- Need direct help? Email us directly at `pilot-support@fashx.studio` or ping your pilot liaison on WhatsApp. We reply within 24 hours.
