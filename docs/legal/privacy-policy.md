# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: October 2026  
**Last Updated**: October 11, 2026  
**Applicable Jurisdiction**: India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)  
**Contact**: `privacy@fashx.studio` | Data Protection Officer: `dpo@fashx.studio`  

---

## 1. At a Glance (Plain English Summary)

At FashXStudio, we believe your personal body photos and personal style are deeply private. Here is our direct commitment to you:
- **Your photos are used for one thing only**: Generating your virtual try-on previews.
- **Zero Public AI Training**: We **never** sell your photos, and we **never** use your photos to train public generative AI foundation models.
- **You are in full control**: You can delete your photo and your entire account instantly with one click directly inside the app.
- **Automated Cleanup**: If you try on clothes and don't save the look to your closet, the try-on images and records are permanently deleted automatically after **30 days**.
- **Data Stored in India**: All primary databases, logs, and user photos are stored securely in AWS Mumbai (`ap-south-1`).

---

## 2. What Information We Collect

1. **Account Details**: Your email address (used for one-time password sign-in).
2. **Self-Portrait / Body Photos**: Photos you capture or upload to visualize how garments look on you.
3. **Fit & Style Preferences**: Size selections (e.g., S, M, L), body height, and style preferences.
4. **App Interaction**: Liked, saved, or hidden clothing items to personalize your feed recommendations.
5. **Technical Logs**: Error diagnostics and network security logs, scrubbed of personal identifiers.

---

## 3. How We Process Your Photos

When you request a virtual try-on:
1. Your photo is securely transmitted over encrypted HTTPS to our virtual try-on processing pipeline (licensed Apache-2.0 models / FASHN AI hosted inference).
2. The model renders the garment onto your silhouette.
3. The resulting image is delivered exclusively to your device.
4. The processing vendor processes the image ephemerally and deletes it from cache within 24 hours.

---

## 4. Your Rights Under Indian Law (DPDP Act 2023)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights:
- **Right to Access (Section 11)**: Request a full summary and export of all personal data we hold about you.
- **Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12)**: Request immediate correction of incorrect data or total erasure of your account.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent**: Revoke your consent for photo processing at any time under Settings.
- **Right to Grievance Redressal**: Contact our Data Protection Officer at `dpo@fashx.studio`. We respond to all requests within 72 hours.

---

## 5. Account Deletion & Permanent Erasure

You can permanently delete your account at any time:
1. Open the FashX app > **Profile** > **Settings** > **Privacy & Rights** > **Delete Account**.
2. Deleting your account immediately purges your photos from cloud storage, deletes your profile records, strips identifiers from analytics, and issues a cryptographic tombstone to prevent revival.
