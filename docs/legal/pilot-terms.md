# Pilot Participant Agreement & Terms

**Program**: FashXStudio Closed Beta / Pilot (October - November 2026)  
**Eligibility**: Invited participants aged 18+ resident in India  
**Target Pilot Size**: 100 Invited Users  

---

## 1. Nature of the Pilot Program

Thank you for participating as an invited tester in the FashXStudio pilot program.
- **Pre-Release Software**: You acknowledge that FashXStudio is experimental, pre-release software currently undergoing closed evaluation. Features may change, and occasional latency, service degradation, or software bugs may occur.
- **Voluntary Participation**: Participation in this pilot is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw from the pilot and delete your account at any time without penalty.

---

## 2. Photo Processing & Third-Party AI Inference During Pilot

- **Virtual Try-On Processing**: As part of testing the styling features, self-portraits you upload will be processed through licensed cloud try-on machine learning APIs (including FASHN AI and AWS Mumbai ECS inference containers).
- **Security & Privacy Commitment**: Your photos are processed exclusively to generate your preview outfits. Neither FashXStudio nor our technology processors use your photos to train public foundation models or retain them beyond the pilot operational retention period (unsaved try-on images are deleted after 30 days).

---

## 3. Feedback & Data Sharing

- **Product Feedback**: You agree to provide occasional qualitative feedback, fit satisfaction ratings, and bug reports through our in-app feedback modal or pilot communication channel.
- **Confidentiality**: Features, prototypes, and unreleased designs shown within the pilot app are proprietary. You agree not to distribute public screenshots or recordings to social media or press without prior written permission from FashXStudio.

---

## 4. Termination & Account Deletion

You retain full rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You can initiate full account deletion at any time via the in-app menu. Upon deletion, all biometric captures and profile entries are purged permanently within seconds.
