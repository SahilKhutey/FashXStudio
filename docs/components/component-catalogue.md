# Component Catalogue
13 Platform-Approved Components:
1. `restaurant-header`: Top cover, logo, address, hours banner.
2. `category-navigation`: Category switching pills/tabs.
3. `category-section`: Category grouping wrapper.
4. `menu-item-card`: Primary item card with picture and price.
5. `menu-item-detail`: Detailed modal with ingredients and allergens.
6. `search-bar`: Real-time keyword filter.
7. `dietary-filters`: Toggle filters for Vegan, Vegetarian, Gluten-Free.
8. `featured-carousel`: Highlight carousel for chef recommendations.
9. `restaurant-footer`: Legal, allergen disclaimers, and social links.
10. `allergen-badge-group`: Standardized allergen iconography.
11. `price-display`: Currency and promotional price formatting.
12. `order-action-button`: Order CTA button with quantity stepper.
13. `badge-notification`: Highlight badge for new or featured items.
